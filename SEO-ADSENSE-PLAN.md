# FilingBy SEO and AdSense Growth Plan

## 1. Realistic traffic target

`1 lakh+ visitors per week` means roughly `14,000+ visitors per day`.
That is possible only if the site ranks for many high-intent pages, not only the homepage.

The main growth pages should be:

- `CA/compliance pages`: GST, ITR, company registration, trademark, ROC, MSME, FSSAI
- `virtual office pages`: city pages, area pages, use-case pages, ecommerce VPOB pages
- `blog pages`: question-based guides that target search intent

## 2. SEO priorities for this project

### Technical SEO

- Keep clean canonicals on every important page
- Keep only indexable public pages inside `sitemap.xml`
- Remove or `noindex` dashboard, admin, callback, and protected pages
- Make sure sitemap URLs exactly match real routes
- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console
- Add Bing Webmaster Tools too

### On-page SEO

Each service page should have:

- one clear `H1`
- 800 to 1,500+ words of useful original content
- pricing, documents, timeline, FAQs, and trust signals
- internal links to related services
- FAQ schema and breadcrumb schema

Each virtual office city page should have:

- city-specific title and description
- local landmarks, business areas, pricing, and verification details
- area pages under the city
- unique content for each city, not copied blocks

## 6. Google AdSense: how it works

AdSense earnings depend mainly on:

- traffic volume
- traffic country
- keyword intent
- page RPM
- ad placement quality

Formula:

`earnings = pageviews x RPM / 1000`

Example:

- `100,000 pageviews/month`
- `RPM Rs. 80`
- earnings about `Rs. 8,000/month`

If traffic is mostly India and mixed-intent, RPM is often modest.
For this niche, direct lead generation usually earns much more than AdSense.

## 7. How to earn more than AdSense

For FilingBy, highest revenue usually comes from:

- service leads
- WhatsApp inquiries
- quote requests
- direct bookings
- retargeting users through email/WhatsApp

Priority should be:

1. rank pages
2. capture lead
3. convert service sale
4. use AdSense lightly on blog pages only

## 8. Best AdSense placement for this project

Use ads mostly on blog and informational pages.
Avoid clutter on service conversion pages.

Recommended:

- in-article ads on blogs
- one ad below blog intro
- one ad mid-content
- one ad before related articles

Avoid:

- too many ads above the fold
- ads inside forms
- ads that push CTA buttons away
- heavy ads on money pages like checkout/service pages

## 10. Most important truth

Traffic does not come from meta tags alone.
To reach `1 lakh+ weekly visitors`, the winning system is:

- many indexable pages
- strong search intent matching
- original content
- internal linking
- consistent publishing
- backlinks
- conversion-first design

For this project, SEO should focus on `service + city + blog` pages first, and AdSense should stay secondary to lead generation.
