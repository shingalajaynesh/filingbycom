# FSSAI Labelling Compliance Guide: Mandatory Declarations, Nutritional Panels & 2026 Norms

In India's competitive consumer packaged goods (CPG) and direct-to-consumer (D2C) food sector, product packaging serves as both a primary sales vehicle and a legally binding regulatory document. While marketing teams focus on vibrant color palettes, typography, and brand storytelling, regulatory compliance officers must ensure that every square centimeter of label real estate satisfies the stringent statutory provisions enforced by the **Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)**.

Printing non-compliant labels is an expensive disaster. Under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSA)**, an improperly labeled product is categorized as **"Misbranded Food"** under **Section 3(1)(zf)**. This empowers food safety officers to issue product recalls, seize warehouse inventory, and levy fines up to ₹3,00,000 per violation under **Section 52**.

This guide details the mandatory declarations required under the **Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020**, nutritional information panels, allergen disclosures, and the intersection with the **Legal Metrology Act**.

---

## The Regulatory Charter: FSSAI Labelling Regulations

Every pre-packaged food item manufactured, imported, or re-packaged for sale in India is governed by the **Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020**, alongside the **FSS (Advertising and Claims) Regulations, 2018**.

The primary legal objective is absolute consumer transparency: ensuring purchasers are accurately informed about product identity, exact ingredients, nutritional density, potential allergens, and shelf-life boundaries without deceptive claims.

---

## The Mandatory 12-Point Declaration Checklist

Under Regulation 5, every pre-packaged food container must carry the following mandatory declarations:

### MANDATORY 12-POINT FSSAI PACKAGING CHECKLIST

| **1. Legal Name of the Food** | **2. List of Ingredients (Descending)** |
| --- | --- |
| 3. Nutritional Information Panel | 4. Veg / Non-Veg Geometric Symbol |
| 5. Food Additives (INS Numbers) | 6. Allergen Warning Statement |
| 7. Name & Address (Mfg/Packer) | 8. FSSAI Logo & 14-Digit Licence No |
| 9. Net Quantity (Legal Metrology) | 10. Batch, Lot, or Code Number |
| 11. Date of Mfg & Expiry / Best | 12. Country of Origin (If Imported) |


---

## Nutritional Information Panel Architecture

Under Regulation 5(3), nutritional information must be presented in a structured tabular format per **100 grams (100g)** or per **100 milliliters (100ml)** of the food, and per **single serving size**:

### Mandatory Nutrient Disclosures:
1. **Energy Value** (expressed in kilocalories - kcal).
2. **Protein** (expressed in grams - g).
3. **Carbohydrate** (expressed in grams - g), with an explicit sub-line item showing **Total Sugars** (g) and **Added Sugars** (g).
4. **Total Fat** (expressed in grams - g), with sub-line items showing **Saturated Fat** (g) and **Trans Fat** (other than naturally occurring trans fat, in g).
5. **Sodium** (expressed in milligrams - mg).
6. **Per-Serving Percentage Contribution to Recommended Dietary Allowance (% RDA)**: Calculated based on the standard 2,000 kcal diet established by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR-NIN).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Trans Fat Ceiling**: Under current statutory regulations, the trans fat content in fats and oils, as well as in foods containing partially hydrogenated oils, cannot exceed **2% by weight**. If trans fat is less than 0.2g per serving, the claim "Trans Fat Free" may be made.

## Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian Distinctive Logos

To protect religious, ethical, and cultural dietary practices, Indian law enforces strict visual symbols on all food packs:

### VEG VS NON-VEG STATUTORY LOGO RULES

| **1. Vegetarian Food Symbol** | **2. Non-Vegetarian Food Symbol** |
| --- | --- |
| Green filled circle | Brown filled triangle |
| Inside a green square frame | Inside a brown square frame |
| RGB: 0, 140, 68 | RGB: 121, 68, 59 |
| Geometric Distinction: The brown triangle design ensures individuals |  |
| with red-green color blindness can easily distinguish non-veg foods. |  |


* **Size Guidelines**: The dimensions of the square frame and internal symbol depend on the surface area of the Principal Display Panel (PDP). For packs up to 100 sq cm, the square side must be at least 3 mm; for packs exceeding 500 sq cm, the side must be at least 8 mm.

## Mandatory Allergen Declaration Protocols

Food allergies represent an acute medical risk. Under Regulation 5(4), if a food product contains any of the following eight recognized allergens (either as an ingredient or through shared manufacturing lines), it must be prominently declared:

1. **Cereals containing gluten** (wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt).
2. **Crustaceans** and products thereof.
3. **Eggs** and egg products.
4. **Fish** and fish products.
5. **Peanuts, tree nuts** (almonds, walnuts, cashews) and products thereof.
6. **Soybeans** and soy products.
7. **Milk and milk products** (including lactose).
8. **Sulfite** in concentrations of 10 mg/kg or more.

### Formatting Standard:
The allergen statement must be printed in **bold capital letters** immediately following the ingredient list:
> **CONTAINS: WHEAT, SOY, AND MILK. MAY CONTAIN TRACES OF PEANUTS.**

## FSSAI Logo and 14-Digit Licence Display Specifications

The FSSAI emblem provides regulatory authenticity and must adhere to exact geometric parameters:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  fssai                       │  <-- Official FSSAI Wordmark
│           Lic. No. 12426001000123            │  <-- 14-Digit Licence Number
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Placement**: The logo and licence number must appear on the Principal Display Panel or on the back information panel adjacent to the manufacturer/marketer address.
* **Contrast**: Must be printed in a distinct contrasting color against the background packaging.
* **Third-Party Contract Manufacturing**: If the product is manufactured by a third-party packer and marketed by a brand, **both FSSAI licence numbers must appear**:
  - *"Manufactured by: [Contractor Name], Lic. No. 100..."*
  - *"Marketed by: [Brand Owner Name], Lic. No. 124..."*

## Legal Metrology Intersection: Net Quantity & MRP Rules

FSSAI labelling rules operate in tandem with the **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011**:
* **Maximum Retail Price (MRP)**: Must state *"MRP ₹xx.xx (Inclusive of all taxes)"*.
* **Net Quantity**: Standard metric units (g, kg, ml, L) without misleading packaging sizes.
* **Customer Care Details**: Mandatory name, address, telephone number, and email ID of the designated consumer grievance officer.

## Penalties for Misbranded and Substandard Labelling

Operating with unapproved packaging exposes founders, quality managers, and directors to severe legal liabilities under Chapter X of the FSS Act:

| Offence | Statutory Section | Legal Consequence / Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| **Misbranded Food** | Section 52 | Penalty up to **₹3,00,000** on manufacturer and seller. |
| **Misleading Advertisements & False Claims** | Section 53 | Fine up to **₹10,00,000** and order to issue public corrective retraction. |
| **Food Containing Extraneous Matter** | Section 54 | Penalty up to **₹1,00,000**. |
| **Unsafe Food Leading to Injury / Harm** | Section 59 | Imprisonment from 3 months up to Life, plus fines up to **₹10,00,000**. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the penalty for selling food products with non-compliant or misbranded labels in India?
Under Section 52 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, any person who manufactures, sells, stores, or distributes misbranded food is liable to a statutory penalty which may extend to ₹3,00,000. In cases involving deceptive health claims or misleading marketing, penalties can extend up to ₹10,00,000 under Section 53.

### What is the new symbol for non-vegetarian food products?
Under the Labelling and Display Regulations, the non-vegetarian symbol was updated to a brown filled triangle inside a brown square frame. This geometric distinction from the vegetarian symbol (a green filled circle inside a square) ensures clear visual identification for individuals with red-green color blindness.

### Are imported packaged foods required to carry the FSSAI logo and Indian importer details?
Yes. All imported pre-packaged foods must display the 14-digit FSSAI licence number and registered trade address of the Indian importer. Importers may affix a non-detachable, tamper-evident sticker before customs clearance containing the mandatory Indian statutory declarations.

### Can a food brand use terms like 'Natural', 'Fresh', or 'Pure' on its packaging?
Under the Food Safety and Standards (Advertising and Claims) Regulations, words such as 'Natural', 'Fresh', 'Pure', or 'Authentic' cannot be used arbitrarily. They can only be claimed if the food satisfies rigorous statutory criteria (e.g., zero additives, unprocessed state, or single-origin agricultural sourcing).

### Where must the FSSAI logo and licence number be placed on the package?
Under the regulations, the FSSAI logo and 14-digit licence number must be displayed on the principal display panel or on the information panel of the package, maintaining a contrasting color against the background and meeting minimum font height specifications based on pack surface area.
