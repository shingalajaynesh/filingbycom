# LLP to Private Limited Conversion Guide: Form URC-1, Section 47(xiii) & MCA V3 Process

For many Indian entrepreneurs, launching as a **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** was the smartest possible choice during early bootstrapping. It eliminated statutory audit fees, offered operational agility, and enabled 100% tax-free profit withdrawals under Section 10(2A).

However, as a startup achieves product-market fit and prepares for explosive growth, the limitations of the LLP structure inevitably surface:
* Venture capital (VC) funds refuse to invest unless the entity can issue **Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS)**.
* Senior executive engineering hires demand structured **Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs)**.
* Strategic corporate acquirers prefer equity stock-for-stock acquisitions.

Fortunately, founders do not have to dissolve their LLP, liquidate their business, or abandon their brand history. Under **Chapter XXI, Part I of the Companies Act, 2013**, corporate law provides a statutory mechanism for an operational LLP to convert directly into a **Private Limited Company**.

This guide outlines the statutory procedure under **Section 366**, the critical capital gains tax exemptions under **Section 47(xiii) of the Income Tax Act**, and the end-to-end filing workflow on the **MCA V3 portal**.

## The Statutory Gateway: Chapter XXI Part I (Section 366)

The conversion of an LLP into a company is governed by **Sections 366 through 374 of the Companies Act, 2013**, read with the **Companies (Authorised to Register) Rules, 2014**:

* **Universal Statutory Succession**: Under Section 368, all movable and immovable property, bank accounts, actionable claims, licenses, and intellectual property owned by the LLP automatically vest in the succeeding Private Limited company on the date of conversion.
* **Continuity of Legal Proceedings**: Under Section 369, all pending lawsuits, arbitration references, and statutory proceedings by or against the LLP continue unimpaired, with the company substituted in place of the LLP.

---

## Mandatory Statutory Pre-Requisites Before Conversion

Before initiating webform filings on MCA V3, the LLP must establish complete corporate readiness:

1. **Minimum Number of Partners**: Under Section 366(2), the LLP must have at least **two or more partners** on the date of conversion.
2. **Up-to-Date Annual Filings**: All historical annual returns (**Form 11**) and financial statements (**Form 8**) must be fully filed and approved by the ROC. An LLP with unfiled returns cannot proceed.
3. **Unanimous Partner Consent**: Hold a formal partner meeting and pass a unanimous resolution (or at least a three-fourths majority as provided in the LLP Agreement) consenting to the conversion.
4. **Secured Creditor No Objection Certificates (NOCs)**: Secure written NOCs from all banks, financial institutions, and secured lenders holding charges against the LLP's assets.
5. **Statement of Accounts (15-Day Rule)**: Prepare an audited balance sheet and profit & loss account of the LLP prepared not earlier than **15 days prior to the date of application**, certified by an independent Chartered Accountant.

## Securing Tax Neutrality Under Section 47(xiii) of the Income Tax Act

The single greatest financial hazard of converting an LLP is inadvertently triggering **Capital Gains Tax**. Under general tax principles, transferring business assets from one legal person (LLP) to another (Private Limited Company) is treated as a taxable sale.

However, **Section 47(xiii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961** provides that such succession shall **NOT be regarded as a transfer**, provided the following statutory conditions are strictly met:

### SECTION 47(xiii) TAX-FREE CONVERSION CONDITIONS

| **1. All assets and liabilities of the LLP immediately before succession** |
| --- |
| become the assets and liabilities of the succeeding company. |
| 2. All partners of the LLP become shareholders of the company in the |
| EXACT SAME PROPORTION as their capital accounts stood in the LLP. |
| 3. The partners receive NO consideration or benefit, directly or |
| indirectly, other than by allotment of equity shares. (No cash!). |
| 4. The aggregate shareholding of the LLP partners must NOT fall below |
| 50% of the total voting power in the company for at least 5 YEARS. |


> [!CAUTION]
> If the founding partners dilute their combined shareholding below 50% within 5 years of conversion, or if any partner is paid cash consideration during the conversion, the **Section 47(xiii) exemption is revoked retrospectively**. The entire capital gain on the market valuation of the business becomes taxable in the hands of the company in the year of default.

## Step-by-Step Conversion Procedure on MCA V3

The conversion workflow combines Chapter XXI filings with standard SPICe+ incorporation:

```
[Step 1: Name Reservation via Web Form RUN]
  Apply for the name on MCA V3. The name must be identical to the LLP name,
  with the suffix "LLP" replaced by "Private Limited".
        │
        ▼
[Step 2: Publish Public Notice in Form URC-2]
  Publish advertisements in one English and one vernacular local newspaper.
  Wait 21 calendar days for public / creditor objections.
        │
        ▼
[Step 3: Draft Form URC-1 (Application for Registration under Chapter XXI)]
  Attach List of Partners, 15-day Audited Accounts, URC-2 Clippings,
  Creditor NOCs, Statement of Assets/Liabilities, and Partner Affidavits.
        │
        ▼
[Step 4: Concurrently Draft Integrated SPICe+ (INC-32)]
  - Draft electronic Memorandum of Association (e-MoA INC-33)
  - Draft electronic Articles of Association (e-AoA INC-34)
  - Complete AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35) for EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax
  - Complete Form INC-9 (Director declarations)
        │
        ▼
[Step 5: Sign with Class 3 DSCs & Submit to CRC]
        │
        ▼
[Central Registration Centre Approves -> Issues Certificate of Incorporation (CoI)]
```

## Form URC-2: Newspaper Publication and Creditor Objections

Under **Rule 3(2) of the Companies (Authorised to Register) Rules, 2014**, public transparency is mandatory before corporate veil restructuring:
* **Publication Sockets**: The notice must be published in **Form URC-2** in two leading daily newspapers circulating in the district where the registered office is located:
  - One newspaper in the English language.
  - One newspaper in the principal vernacular language of the district.
* **The 21-Day Objection Window**: Any person, vendor, or creditor having any objection to the conversion has 21 days from the date of publication to submit written objections to the jurisdictional Registrar of Companies.
* **Affidavit of Compliance**: A copy of the printed newspaper clippings, along with an affidavit by the designated partners confirming publication, forms a mandatory attachment to Form URC-1.

## Post-Conversion Legal and Regulatory Transitions

Upon approval, the ROC issues a **Certificate of Incorporation** with a 21-character Corporate Identity Number (CIN). The legal transformation triggers essential administrative transitions:

| System / Asset | Statutory Transition Protocol |
|---|---|
| **PAN & TAN** | MCA issues a fresh corporate PAN and TAN automatically on the CoI. |
| **GST Transition (ITC-02)** | Obtain a new GSTIN under the company PAN. File **Form GST ITC-02** to transfer unutilized Input Tax Credit from the LLP's credit ledger to the new company. |
| **Bank Account Migration** | Transition current accounts from LLP name to Private Limited name, updating board resolutions and signing mandates. |
| **Vendor & Client Contracts** | Issue formal contract novation addendums or letters of continuity under Section 368 to all existing clients. |
| **Trademark Records** | File Form TM-P with the Trademark Registry to record the succession in ownership of all registered trademarks. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can an LLP convert into a Private Limited Company without paying capital gains tax?
Yes. Under Section 47(xiii) of the Income Tax Act, the transfer of assets from an LLP to a succeeding Private Limited company is completely exempt from capital gains tax, provided all assets and liabilities are transferred, all partners become shareholders in the exact proportion of their capital accounts, and they retain at least 50% voting power for a minimum of 5 years.

### Is newspaper advertisement mandatory for converting an LLP into a company?
Yes. Under Rule 3(2) of the Companies (Authorised to Register) Rules, 2014, the partners must publish a notice in Form URC-2 in two leading newspapers (one in English and one in the principal vernacular language of the district where the registered office is located) at least 21 days before filing Form URC-1.

### Can an LLP with pending annual filings (Form 8 or Form 11) apply for conversion?
No. The Registrar of Companies (ROC) will summarily reject Form URC-1 if the LLP has any overdue annual compliance filings. All pending Form 11 (Annual Return) and Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency) filings must be cleared on MCA V3 before applying.

### What happens to the existing contracts, leases, and intellectual property of the LLP after conversion?
Under Section 368 of the Companies Act, 2013, all properties, movable and immovable, and all debts, liabilities, and obligations of the LLP automatically vest in the newly incorporated Private Limited Company upon conversion. Existing commercial contracts continue seamlessly by operation of law.

### Does the converted company receive a new PAN and GSTIN?
Yes. Because a Private Limited Company is a distinct corporate legal person from an LLP under tax statutes, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs issues a fresh Corporate Identity Number (CIN) and a new corporate PAN. The company must then apply for a new GSTIN and transfer input tax credit via Form GST ITC-02.
