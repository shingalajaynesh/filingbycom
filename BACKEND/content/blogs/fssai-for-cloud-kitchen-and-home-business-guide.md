# FSSAI for Cloud Kitchens & Home Food Businesses: Swiggy/Zomato Rules, 2026 Slabs & Schedule 4

The food delivery sector in India has undergone a massive structural transformation. Driven by consumer delivery apps like **Swiggy and Zomato**, entrepreneurs no longer require prime retail street fronts, high-rent dining halls, or extensive front-of-house staff. **Cloud kitchens** (also known as dark kitchens, ghost kitchens, or virtual restaurants) and **home-based culinary businesses** can serve thousands of customers directly from lean, delivery-optimized production spaces.

However, operating in a virtual format does not exempt a food business from statutory health and safety oversight. Under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (FSSA)**, any entity that prepares, packages, or distributes food intended for human consumption must be licensed by the **Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)**.

This practical operational guide explains the licensing tiers under the **modernized 2026 thresholds**, aggregator onboarding rules, the legal framework for multi-brand dark kitchens, and mandatory **Schedule 4 hygiene standards**.

## The Rise of Dark Kitchens & Home Food Delivery

A cloud kitchen is a commercial facility built strictly for delivery and takeaway, with zero customer dine-in facilities. While this model drastically lowers real estate overhead, it introduces specific regulatory touchpoints:

| **1. Home-Based Food Venture** | **2. Standalone Dark Kitchen** |

Regardless of which model you operate, food safety authorities treat consumer risk identically: unhygienic preparation, pest contamination, or improper temperature controls carry severe statutory penalties.

## FSSAI Registration vs Licence: The 2026 Thresholds

On **April 1, 2026**, the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare updated the FSSAI turnover criteria:

### 1. FSSAI Basic Registration (Turnover Up to ₹1.5 Crores)
* **Who it covers**: Petty food business operators, micro-caterers, and home kitchens with gross annual sales **up to ₹1.5 Crores** (substantially expanded from the previous ₹12 Lakhs ceiling).
* **FoSCoS Filing**: **Form A**.
* **Statutory Fee**: Standardized at **₹100 per year**.
* **Suitability**: Perfectly suited for home bakers, regional snack startups, and early-stage cloud kitchen pilots testing their menu.

### 2. FSSAI State Licence (Turnover > ₹1.5 Crores Up to ₹50 Crores)
* **Who it covers**: Commercial cloud kitchens, multi-brand dark kitchen hubs, and catering commissaries with revenue **between ₹1.5 Crores and ₹50 Crores**.
* **FoSCoS Filing**: **Form B**.
* **Statutory Fee**: Slabs between **₹2,000 and ₹5,000 per year**, depending on installed kitchen capacity and electrical equipment.
* **Requirements**: Mandates NABL-accredited water laboratory testing reports, kitchen layout blueprints, and commercial lease deeds.

## Aggregator Onboarding Mandates (Swiggy & Zomato)

Under the **Food Safety and Standards (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Amendment Regulations**, online delivery platforms are legally designated as **E-Commerce Food Business Operators (E-Commerce FBOs)**.

As part of their statutory obligations:
1. **Mandatory License Gatekeeping**: Aggregators are strictly barred from activating a merchant store or listing a menu without validating an active 14-digit FSSAI number against the FoSCoS database.
2. **Public Display of FSSAI Number**: Under FSSAI consumer protection directives, the aggregator must display the cloud kitchen's 14-digit FSSAI licence number on the restaurant’s digital listing page right next to its rating and hygiene badge.
3. **Automated Deactivation**: If an FBO fails to pay its annual maintenance fees on FoSCoS, the portal marks the licence as suspended. The aggregator’s automated API integrations delist the restaurant within 24 hours.

## Licensing Multi-Brand Cloud Kitchens Under One Roof

A common commercial practice is the "multi-brand dark kitchen"—for instance, preparing pizzas under Brand A, biryani under Brand B, and milkshakes under Brand C from the exact same cooking line.

Founders often mistakenly assume they must buy three separate FSSAI licences.

```
[Physical Commercial Facility: Unit 104, Industrial Estate]
                            │
                            ▼
     [Single Primary FSSAI Licence Issued for the Premises]
                            │
       ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
       ▼                    ▼                    ▼
[Brand A: Pizza Co.] [Brand B: Biryani Hub] [Brand C: Shake Bar]
  Listed on FoSCoS     Listed on FoSCoS     Listed on FoSCoS
  under "Trade Names"  under "Trade Names"  under "Trade Names"
```

### The FoSCoS Endorsement Rule:
You need only **one physical FSSAI licence** for the kitchen premises. However, in the FoSCoS application, you must declare every digital brand in the **Trade Names** section. Each aggregator onboarding team cross-references the trade name on the menu with the FoSCoS certificate. If a brand name is missing, the platform will refuse onboarding.

## Schedule 4 Sanitary & Hygiene Requirements

Every food preparation unit must adhere to the **General Hygienic and Sanitary Practices** codified in **Schedule 4 of the FSS (Licensing and Registration) Regulations**:

1. **Physical Kitchen Infrastructure**:
   - Floors, walls, and ceilings must be smooth, non-absorbent, and easily cleanable (ceramic tiling recommended up to 6 feet).
   - Windows and exhaust openings must be fitted with fly-proof insect mesh screens.
   - Commercial kitchen hoods with grease filters must be installed above deep fryers, tandoors, and stoves.
2. **Equipment & Utensils**:
   - Food-grade stainless steel (SS-304) preparation tables and chopping boards (color-coded boards for raw meat, poultry, vegetables, and cooked food to avoid cross-contamination).
3. **Pest Control Discipline**:
   - Formal pest control contracts with licensed exterminators, maintaining service visit logs and pesticide safety data sheets.
4. **Food Safety Display Board (FSDB)**:
   - Mandatory installation of an **FSSAI Purple Display Board** (designated for restaurants and cloud kitchens) detailing basic food safety golden rules and the kitchen's licence number.

## Operating from Residential Premises: Society NOC & Clearances

For home bakers and tiffin suppliers, operating from a domestic residence offers low setup costs. However, founders must navigate local civic boundaries:

* **Housing Society / RWA No Objection Certificate**:
  FSSAI authorities in many municipal jurisdictions require an NOC from the housing society or resident welfare association confirming that cooking and delivery traffic will not create a public nuisance.
* **Commercial Power & Gas**:
  While domestic LPG cylinders are subsidized for domestic cooking, utilizing them for commercial baking or packaging violates the Essential Commodities Act. Operators must transition to commercial LPG cylinders once volume expands.
* **Local Municipal Trade Licences**:
  Certain municipal corporations (such as BBMP in Bengaluru or BMC in Mumbai) require a separate Health Trade Licence for commercial food operations, even if operating on an FSSAI Basic Registration.

## Mandatory Medical Fitness (Form IX) & Water Testing

Under FSSAI audit protocols, kitchen compliance hinges on two vital health records:

### 1. Annual Medical Fitness Certificates (Form IX)
Every employee handling food, packaging, or kitchen utensils must be medically certified once every 12 months by a registered medical practitioner (MBBS). The examination verifies:
- Active immunization against Typhoid.
- Absence of infectious tuberculosis, cholera, and skin lesions.
- Routine stool examination for enteric pathogens.

### 2. Potable Water Testing (IS 10500 Standards)
Water used for cooking, washing utensils, and ice preparation must conform to **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) IS 10500:2012**. For State Licences, FBOs must upload an analytical test report from an accredited NABL laboratory testing for pH, total dissolved solids (TDS), coliform bacteria, and heavy metals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a home baker sell cakes and snacks on Swiggy or Zomato using an FSSAI Basic Registration?
Yes. As long as the home baker's annual turnover remains below ₹1.5 Crores (effective April 1, 2026), an FSSAI Basic Registration (Form A) is completely valid for onboarding on Swiggy, Zomato, and other digital delivery platforms.

### If I operate four virtual brands from one dark kitchen, do I need four separate FSSAI licences?
No. Food licensing attaches to the physical food preparation facility, not the digital brand name. You need only one FSSAI State or Basic licence for the physical premises. However, you must declare all virtual trade names in the FoSCoS application to ensure each brand reconciles during aggregator onboarding.

### Can I run a commercial cloud kitchen from a rented residential flat?
Operating a high-volume commercial cloud kitchen from a residential apartment frequently faces local municipal zoning restrictions and residential society objections. However, small-scale home baking and tiffin services are permissible provided you obtain a written No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the landlord and housing society.

### What is Form IX medical fitness certification for kitchen staff?
Under Schedule 4 of the FSS Regulations, every person handling food must undergo an annual medical examination by a registered medical practitioner. Form IX certifies that food handlers are vaccinated against typhoid and free from communicable diseases, skin infections, and tuberculosis.

### Is a potable water test report mandatory for cloud kitchen licensing?
For an FSSAI State Licence (turnover > ₹1.5 Crores), submitting a water testing report from a NABL-accredited or FSSAI-notified laboratory confirming compliance with IS 10500 drinking water standards is mandatory. For Basic Registration, a self-declaration of potable water source is generally accepted.
