# Company Name Approval in India: Rule 8, MCA V3 Search & SPICe+ Part A Guide

Choosing a corporate name is one of the foundational decisions a founder makes. Your company name is not merely a legal label; it appears on client contracts, enterprise invoices, trademark journals, bank accounts, and investor cap tables.

However, many founders discover that the Central Registration Centre (CRC) rejects their creative brand names.

The **Companies Act, 2013** and **Rule 8 and 8A of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014** establish rigorous legal standards to ensure no corporate name is misleading, identical, or deceptively similar to existing companies or registered trademarks.

Understanding these statutory rules—and conducting preliminary checks on both the **MCA database** and the **IP India trademark portal**—is essential to secure name approval on your very first submission.

---

## The Three-Part Anatomy of a Valid Company Name

Under Indian corporate law, a compliant company name consists of three structural components:

1. **Distinctive Prefix (Unique / Coined Word)**
2. **Descriptive Noun (Activity Word Indicating Industry Object)**
3. **Statutory Suffix ("Private Limited" or "OPC Private Limited")**

- **Example 1:** `Aethel` *(Distinctive)* + `Aero Technologies` *(Activity)* + `Private Limited` *(Suffix)*.
- **Example 2:** `Kavach` *(Distinctive)* + `Cyber Security` *(Activity)* + `Private Limited` *(Suffix)*.

*Why Activity Words Matter:* Under Rule 8(2)(b), the name must reflect the primary object stated in Clause 3 of the Memorandum of Association. Naming a software venture *"Apex Ventures Private Limited"* without an activity word like *"Technologies"* or *"Digital"* frequently triggers a query requesting an explicit object indicator.

---

## Rule 8: Undesirable Names & Tests of Similarity

Under Rule 8(1), a proposed name is considered identical or deceptively similar to an existing company if the difference merely consists of:

### 1. Plural or Singular Variations
- If *Alpha Healthcare Pvt Ltd* exists, you cannot register *Alphas Healthcare Pvt Ltd*.

### 2. Punctuation, Spacing, or Minor Phonetic Tweaks
- If *QuickTech Solutions Pvt Ltd* exists, you cannot register *Quick-Tech Solutions Pvt Ltd* or *KwikTech Solutions Pvt Ltd*.

### 3. Tense or Combinations of Words
- If *Bright Light Software Pvt Ltd* exists, *Brightening Light Software Pvt Ltd* is legally deemed identical.

### 4. Addition of Place Names
- If *Veloce Motors Pvt Ltd* exists, you cannot bypass it by applying for *Veloce Motors India Pvt Ltd* or *Veloce Motors Delhi Pvt Ltd*, unless you can prove corporate parentage.

### 5. Translation into Another Language
- Translating an existing company's name into Hindi or an Indian regional language is deemed identical (e.g., *National Steel* vs *Rashtriya Steel*).

## Rule 8A: Trademark Conflicts and Prior Brand Rights

One of the most frequent reasons for rejection by the CRC is a conflict with a registered or pending trademark under **Rule 8A**:

```
[Founder Proposes Name in SPICe+ Part A]
                    │
                    ▼
[CRC Examiner Searches MCA Company Database]
                    │
                    ▼
[CRC Examiner Searches IP India Trademark Database (Classes 1–45)]
                    │
   ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
   ▼                                 ▼
[No Trademark Conflicts Found]   [Identical Trademark Exists in Same Industry]
   │                                 │
   ▼                                 ▼
[Name Approved]                  [Resubmission / Rejection Notice Issued]
                                 (Requires NOC from Trademark Owner)
```

If a third party holds a registered trademark for *Zepron* in Class 9 or 42 (software), an applicant cannot incorporate *Zepron Technologies Private Limited* without an official **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** and Board Resolution from the trademark proprietor.

## Prohibited Words and Words Requiring Regulatory Clearance

### 1. Absolute Statutory Prohibitions
Under the Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950, names cannot contain:
- Names or emblems of the President, Prime Minister, Governor, or Government of India.
- Words suggesting patronage of Central or State Governments (*"National"*, *"Federal"*, *"Union"*, *"Central"*, *"Rashtrapati"*).
- The name of any foreign government or international body (*"United Nations"*, *"WHO"*).

### 2. Words Requiring Prior Sectoral Approval
Certain words require prior written in-principle clearance from specialized regulators before the CRC will accept the name:
- **Banking / Finance:** *"Bank"*, *"Banking"*, *"Venture Capital"*, *"Asset Management"* (Requires RBI / SEBI clearance).
- **Insurance:** *"Insurance"*, *"Assurance"*, *"Reinsurance"* (Requires IRDAI clearance).
- **Stock Exchange / Broking:** *"Stock Exchange"*, *"Depository"* (Requires SEBI clearance).

## Step-by-Step Procedure to File SPICe+ Part A

1. **Pre-Filing Verification:**
   - Run a company master data search on `mca.gov.in` to check existing company and LLP names.
   - Run a wordmark and phonetic search on `tmrpublicsearch.ipindia.gov.in` across relevant NICE classes.
2. **Access SPICe+ Part A on MCA V3:** Log into your business user account and select **"SPICe+ Part A"**.
3. **Select Entity Class:** Choose *Type of Company (New Company)*, *Class of Company (Private)*, and *Category (Company limited by shares)*.
4. **Input Name Choices:** Enter up to **two proposed names** in order of priority.
5. **Main Objects Summary:** Enter a concise 200-to-300 word summary of the commercial activities your startup will execute.
6. **Upload Attachments (If Applicable):** If using a promoter's registered trademark or foreign parent company name, attach the formal NOC and board resolution.
7. **Statutory Fee Payment:** Settle the official filing fee of **₹1,000** online.

---

## Handling Resubmission Notices (The 15-Day Cure Period)

If the CRC officer finds an objection under Rule 8:
- The status updates to **"Resubmission"**.
- The portal gives you **15 days** to respond.
- You can provide legal justifications explaining why your name is distinct, OR submit **two completely fresh alternative names** without paying another ₹1,000 fee.
- If the resubmitted names are also rejected, the application is closed as **"Rejected"**, and a fresh ₹1,00,00 filing fee must be paid.

## SPICe+ Part A vs RUN: When to Use Which

| Parameter | SPICe+ Part A | RUN (Reserve Unique Name) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Use Case** | Name reservation for **incorporating a new company** | Name change for an **already existing active company** |
| **Number of Names Allowed** | **2 names** per submission | **2 names** per submission |
| **Resubmission Window** | 1 free resubmission cycle (15 days) | 1 free resubmission cycle (15 days) |
| **Government Filing Fee** | **₹1,000** | **₹1,000** |
| **Validity Upon Approval** | **20 days** to file SPICe+ Part B | **60 days** from date of approval to file change |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I register a company name that consists of generic dictionary words?
No. Names consisting purely of generic or descriptive terms (e.g., 'Fast Logistics Private Limited' or 'Best Software Solutions Private Limited') are rejected under Rule 8(2)(d) for lacking distinctive character, unless preceded by an invented, coined, or unique prefix.

### How many names can I apply for in SPICe+ Part A?
An applicant can submit up to two proposed names in order of preference in a single SPICe+ Part A filing for the standard ₹1,000 government fee. If both names are queried, the portal allows one free resubmission cycle with two fresh names.

### Does company name approval give me exclusive trademark protection?
No. Company name approval by the MCA only prevents another entity from incorporating under that exact corporate name on the MCA register. It does not grant proprietary trademark rights under the Trade Marks Act 1999; you must file a separate trademark application (Form TM-A) for trademark exclusivity.

### Can an existing company change its name using SPICe+ Part A?
No. SPICe+ Part A is strictly for incorporating new companies. Existing corporate entities changing their name must file the RUN (Reserve Unique Name) web-form on the MCA portal.
