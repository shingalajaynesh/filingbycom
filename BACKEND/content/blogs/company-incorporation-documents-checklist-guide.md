# Company Incorporation Documents Checklist: What Founders Must Prepare for SPICe+

When incorporating an Indian Private Limited Company via the integrated **SPICe+ (INC-32)** web form on the **MCA V3 portal**, speed is almost entirely a function of document hygiene.

The **Central Registration Centre (CRC)** at Manesar processes thousands of incorporation forms daily through automated optical character recognition (OCR) and manual examiner review. If a utility bill is 65 days old, a landlord's name on a No Objection Certificate (NOC) has a spelling discrepancy, or a director's bank statement scan is blurry, the application is halted with a **Resubmission Query**.

Having an unshakeable, verified document dossier prepared *before* initiating the online application ensures smooth, first-pass approval within 5 to 7 working days.

This guide provides an exhaustive, rejection-proof checklist of every document required for promoters, directors, and the corporate registered office.

---

## Master Incorporation Document Architecture

### COMPANY INCORPORATION DOSSIER

| **1. Promoter &** | **Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card** |
| --- | --- |
| Director Proofs | Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Passport / License Bank Statement or Utility Bill (< 2 months old) Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) |
| 2. Registered | Recent Utility Bill of Premises (< 2 months old) |
| Office Proofs | No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Owner Registered Rent Agreement / Lease Deed / Sale Deed |
| 3. Statutory | Form DIR-2 (Consent to act as Director) |
| Declarations | Form INC-9 (Declaration of Non-Conviction) Specimen Signature Card for Bank Integration |

## Director & Subscriber Identity Proofs

Every founding promoter subscribing to shares and every proposed director must furnish two levels of personal documentation:

### 1. Mandatory Identity Proof
- **For Indian Nationals:** **PAN Card is mandatory**. The name and date of birth printed on the PAN card must match the details entered on the MCA portal character-for-character.
- **For Foreign Nationals:** **Valid International Passport is mandatory**.

### 2. Secondary Identity Proof (Supporting)
Any one of the following government-issued photo identity cards:
- **Aadhaar Card** (Linked with mobile for OTP verification).
- **Voter Identity Card (EPIC)**.
- **Valid Indian Passport**.
- **Valid Driving License**.

---

## Director & Subscriber Address Proofs

To establish residential jurisdiction, each director and subscriber must provide one recent address proof:

| Document Type | Mandatory Requirements for Acceptance | Common Rejection Flaws |
|---|---|---|
| **Bank Account Statement** | Shows complete name, residential address, bank logo, and recent debit/credit transactions. | Statement is older than 2 months, does not display the applicant's address, or is a password-protected PDF. |
| **Electricity Bill (Personal)** | Issued by local power discom in the applicant's name. | In the name of a landlord or parent without supporting proof. |
| **Telephone / Broadband Bill** | Postpaid mobile, landline, or fiber broadband bill in applicant's name. | Prepaid recharge receipts or third-party wallet transaction slips. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The **Two-Month Rule**: Under Rule 16 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014, personal address proofs for directors must not be older than **two months** on the date of submission on MCA V3.

## Registered Office Documentation: The 2-Month Rule

Under Section 12 of the Companies Act, a company must possess a registered office capable of receiving official notices:

### 1. Proof of Address (Utility Bill)
- **Acceptable Documents:** Electricity Bill, Piped Gas Bill, Water Bill, or Telephone/Broadband Bill.
- **Strict Validity:** Must **not be older than 2 months** from the date of filing.
- **Ownership Alignment:** The bill must be in the legal name of the property owner who signs the NOC.
- *Commercial vs Residential:* The address may be a commercial office, co-working facility, or residential residence.

### 2. No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Property Owner
- An explicit declaration by the property owner stating:
  - Full name and address of the owner.
  - Clear survey/flat/plot number of the premises.
  - Affirmative consent allowing the proposed company to register its registered office at the premises without hindrance.

### 3. Proof of Legal Occupancy
- If rented: A copy of the **Rent Agreement or Lease Deed**, supported by rent receipts.
- If owned: A copy of the **Registered Sale Deed, Municipal Tax Receipt, or Khata Certificate**.

## Statutory Consent and Declarations (Form DIR-2 & INC-9)

### 1. Form DIR-2: Consent to Act as Director (Section 152(5))
Each proposed director must execute Form DIR-2 declaring:
- Their willingness to act as director of the proposed entity.
- Disclosure of directorships held in other companies.
- Affirmation that they are not disqualified under Section 164.

### 2. Form INC-9: Subscriber Declaration
An electronic declaration generated dynamically within SPICe+ wherein each subscriber confirms they have not been convicted of any offense relating to the promotion, formation, or management of a company during the preceding five years.

## Special Rules for Foreign Nationals and NRI Directors

If your founding team includes Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) or Foreign Nationals:

1. **Notarization & Apostille under Hague Convention:**
   - If the foreign national resides in a country that is a signatory to the **Hague Apostille Convention, 1961**, all personal identity proofs, address proofs, and signed declarations must be notarized and **apostilled** by the competent authority in that foreign country.
2. **Consular Legalization (Non-Hague Countries):**
   - If residing in a non-Hague nation (e.g., UAE, Singapore, Indonesia), documents must be notarized in that country and subsequently attested by the **Indian Embassy or Consulate**.
3. **Foreign Corporate Subscribers:**
   - If an overseas holding company is subscribing to shares, attach the foreign Certificate of Incorporation, Board Resolution authorizing the Indian investment, and MoA/Charter, all duly apostilled.

## Top 5 Document Inconsistencies That Cause Resubmissions

1. **Name Mismatch between PAN and Bank Statement:** If your PAN states *"Aditya Kumar Sharma"* but your bank statement displays *"Aditya K Sharma"*, the CRC officer will reject the address proof.
2. **Utility Bill Address Omitting City or PIN Code:** Utility bills printed with abbreviated or truncated postal addresses create address mismatches with Form INC-22.
3. **Owner Name in Rent Agreement Not Matching Utility Bill:** If the electricity bill is in the father's name but the rent agreement is signed by the son without an inheritance deed, an ownership defect notice is issued.
4. **Blurry Scans and Illegible Stamps:** Low-resolution camera photos or mobile phone scans under poor lighting are rejected; always upload clean, high-resolution 300 DPI flatbed PDF scans.
5. **Expired Director Passports:** Foreign directors submitting passports with less than 6 months of remaining validity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a residential address be used as the registered office of a Private Limited Company?
Yes. The Companies Act 2013 does not require a commercial storefront. A founder's residential apartment or rented home can legally serve as the registered office, provided a utility bill, rent agreement, and owner NOC are submitted.

### What is the acceptable age of a utility bill for MCA incorporation?
Under Rule 25 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014, the utility bill (electricity bill, landline/broadband bill, water bill, or piped gas bill) must not be older than two months from the date of form filing.

### What happens if a director's name is spelled differently on their PAN and Aadhaar?
The MCA V3 database cross-verifies applicant details in real-time with the Income Tax (PAN) and UIDAI (Aadhaar) databases. Any spelling variation (even a missing middle name or initials) causes an automated pre-scrutiny validation failure. The taxpayer must rectify the PAN or Aadhaar before proceeding.

### Is a bank statement acceptable as a director's address proof?
Yes. A bank statement is the most common address proof for directors and subscribers, provided it shows current residential address, recent transactions, is not older than 2 months, and bears the official bank logo, branch stamp, or verified digital banking seal.
