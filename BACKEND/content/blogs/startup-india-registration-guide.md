# Startup India Registration Guide: Step-by-Step NSWS Portal Filing & Certificate Download

Applying for recognition under the Startup India initiative has been streamlined through the Central Government's **National Single Window System (NSWS)**. By consolidating multiple ministry clearances into a single digital interface, startups can now complete their filing, track examination queries, and download their official **DPIIT Certificate of Recognition** without visiting physical government offices.

This guide provides an exact, step-by-step walkthrough of the digital application process on `nsws.gov.in`, detailing field requirements, attachment specifications, and query resolution protocols.

---

## The Transition to the National Single Window System (NSWS)

Historically, applications were filed directly on the legacy `startupindia.gov.in` portal. Under current operational guidelines, DPIIT registration is routed through the **National Single Window System (NSWS)** (`nsws.gov.in`), which links your company's Corporate Identification Number (CIN) or LLPIN directly with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) database.

---

## Pre-Requisites Before Beginning the Online Application

Ensure you have the following information and files accessible on your workstation:
1. **Corporate Credentials:** Active CIN / LLPIN and entity PAN.
2. **Authorized Signatory Details:** Mobile number and email ID linked with the authorized director's or partner's Aadhaar (for OTP e-signing).
3. **Incorporation Certificate:** Clean PDF copy of Certificate of Incorporation (CoI).
4. **Charter Documents:** Digital copy of MoA & AoA (for Pvt Ltd) or registered LLP Agreement.
5. **Pitch Deck / Concept Note:** PDF file (strictly under 5 MB) detailing the product, innovation, scalability, and job creation.
6. **Web Footprint:** Active URL of your product website, web application, mobile app listing (Play Store/App Store), or unlisted YouTube product walkthrough video.

---

## Step 1: Creating Your Account on the NSWS Portal

1. Navigate to the official NSWS portal: `https://www.nsws.gov.in/`.
2. Click on **"Register"** in the top navigation bar.
3. Select **"Business / Enterprise"** as your user category.
4. Enter your Business Name, Corporate Email, Mobile Number, and PAN.
5. Verify the mobile OTP and email activation link sent by the NSWS authentication gateway.
6. Create a secure password and establish your login credentials.

---

## Step 2: Completing the Common Application Form (CAF)

Once logged into your NSWS dashboard:
1. Access the **Common Application Form (CAF)** module.
2. Enter your entity's **CIN (for Companies)** or **LLPIN (for LLPs)**.
3. Click **"Fetch Details"**. The portal automatically auto-populates your company name, registered office address, date of incorporation, and ROC jurisdiction from the MCA database.
4. Add details of the Authorized Representative (Name, Designation, PAN, Aadhaar number, and email).
5. Save the CAF profile. This Common Profile serves as the master record for all subsequent central approvals.

---

## Step 3: Accessing the DPIIT Startup Recognition Form

1. In the NSWS search bar, type: **"Registration as a Startup (DPIIT)"**.
2. Click on **"Add to Dashboard"** and then select **"Apply Now"**.
3. The system initiates the dedicated DPIIT recognition workflow, syncing your company information from the CAF.

---

## Step 4: Filling Entity Details, Industry Classification & Founders

On the DPIIT registration form, navigate through the required data modules:

1. **Entity Details (Auto-filled from MCA)**
2. **Select Industry, Sector & Sub-Sector**
3. **Enter Promoters / Directors & Shareholding %**
4. **Provide Current Full-Time & Part-Time Headcount**
5. **Specify Prior Funding / Intellectual Property (If any)**

- **Industry & Sector Selection:** Select the sector that accurately reflects your business (e.g., *IT Services > AI & Big Data*, or *Healthcare > Telemedicine*). Choosing an unrelated industry creates mismatch flags during examination.
- **Stage of Startup:** Select between *Ideation, Validation, Early Traction, or Scaling*.
- **Employment Metrics:** Enter the current number of permanent employees and contractors.

---

## Step 5: Submitting the Innovation Questionnaire & Pitch Deck

This section is the core evaluation criteria for the examining committee:

1. **Problem Statement (Max 250 words):** Describe the concrete commercial or technical challenge your product solves.
2. **Proposed Solution & Uniqueness (Max 250 words):** Explain how your proprietary software, hardware design, or business process provides a distinctive solution that does not merely duplicate existing offerings.
3. **Revenue Model & Scalability (Max 250 words):** Detail how the company monetizes (e.g., recurring SaaS subscription, transactional commission, licensing) and how operations can scale geographically without proportional increases in overhead.
4. **Pitch Deck Attachment:** Upload your formatted PDF deck (< 5 MB).
5. **Product / Demo Link:** Provide an active URL to your website, test environment, or video demo.

---

## Step 6: Declaration, Mobile OTP Verification & Final Submission

1. Review the statutory declaration under Notification G.S.R. 127(E), confirming:
   - The entity is not formed by splitting up or reconstructing an existing business.
   - Turnover has not exceeded ₹100 crore in any preceding financial year.
   - The business operates within the 10-year eligibility threshold.
2. Click **"Generate OTP"**. An authentication code is dispatched to the authorized signatory's Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
3. Enter the 6-digit OTP and click **"Submit Application"**.
4. An instant **Application Reference Number (ARN)** is generated on screen and emailed to the account holder.

---

## How to Track Application Status & Resolve Queries

After submission, your application status progresses through defined stages on the NSWS dashboard:

| Status Label | Practical Meaning | Expected Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Submitted / Under Scrutiny** | Application received; queued for digital review by the DPIIT evaluation cell. | No action required. Review takes 3–7 working days. |
| **Resubmission Required / Query Raised** | The evaluating officer requires clarification on documents, pitch deck, or entity structure. | Log in, review specific query text, upload required clarification, and resubmit within **30 days**. |
| **Approved** | Application verified and accepted by the competent authority. | Immediate download of Certificate of Recognition. |
| **Rejected** | Application failed statutory eligibility or innovation tests. | Detailed rejection reason provided. Applicant may reapply with revised data after addressing concerns. |

---

## Downloading and Verifying Your DPIIT Certificate

Once the status indicates **"Approved"**:
1. Open your NSWS dashboard and navigate to **"Completed Approvals"**.
2. Click on **"Download Certificate"**.
3. The issued document is an official government certificate titled **"Certificate of Recognition"** issued by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
4. Key elements on the certificate:
   - **DPIIT Recognition Number** (e.g., `DIPPXXXXX`).
   - Entity Legal Name and Date of Incorporation.
   - Official Digital Seal of the Government of India.
   - Dynamic **QR Code** that redirects to `startupindia.gov.in` for real-time validity verification by banks, patent officers, and tender boards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the official government fee for DPIIT Startup India registration?
The official government fee is ₹0 (Zero). The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade provides recognition completely free of charge on the NSWS portal.

### How long does it take for DPIIT to approve the application?
Standard review typically takes 3 to 7 working days. If all documents, MCA filings, and the innovation pitch deck are clear, certificates are frequently generated within 48 to 72 hours.

### What should I do if my application receives a query from the officer?
Log in to your NSWS dashboard, click on 'My Approvals / Applications', open the query section, read the specific remarks, upload the requested supporting document or clarification letter, and resubmit within 30 days.

### Can I edit my company details on the DPIIT portal after recognition is granted?
Certain fields (such as address or authorized signatory) can be modified by filing an amendment request on the portal with updated MCA records. However, fundamental corporate restructuring requires officer approval.
