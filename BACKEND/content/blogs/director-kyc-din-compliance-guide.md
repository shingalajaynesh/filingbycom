# Director KYC and DIN Compliance Guide: DIR-3 KYC vs Web KYC & Reactivation Rules

Holding a **Director Identification Number (DIN)** or Designated Partner Identification Number (DPIN) is a prerequisite to serving on the board of any Indian Private Limited Company, One Person Company, or Limited Liability Partnership (LLP).

However, many directors assume that once a DIN is allotted by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), it remains active permanently without ongoing maintenance.

Under **Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014**, every individual holding a DIN must undergo **mandatory annual Director KYC verification on or before September 30**.

Overlooking this compliance requirement triggers an automated system flag: your DIN is marked as **"Deactivated due to non-filing of DIR-3 KYC"**, your digital signature is rejected across the entire MCA portal, and the ministry imposes a **flat, non-negotiable statutory late fee of ₹5,000 per director** to reactivate your credentials.

---

## Who Must File DIR-3 KYC Every Year?

The mandate applies to every single individual who holds an allotted DIN as of March 31 of that financial year:
- **Active Directors:** Serving on active corporate boards.
- **Disqualified Directors:** Directors disqualified under Section 164(2) must still file KYC.
- **Dormant or Inactive Directors:** Individuals who obtained a DIN years ago but currently hold no active board seats.
- **Designated Partners in LLPs:** DPINs and DINs share the same database; LLP partners must comply under identical rules.
- **Foreign Directors and NRIs:** Overseas nationals holding Indian DINs must complete annual verification.

---

## Form DIR-3 KYC (e-Form) vs DIR-3 KYC Web: The Core Difference

The MCA provides two distinct workflows depending on whether personal details have changed:

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │    WHICH DIR-3 KYC PROCESS APPLIES TO YOU?    │
                  └───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘
                                          │
                  ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                  ▼                                               ▼
      [FORM DIR-3 KYC: FULL E-FORM]                   [DIR-3 KYC WEB: OTP SERVICE]
      - Filing for the VERY FIRST time                - Subsequent years after initial e-form
      - Personal details CHANGED                      - NO details changed since last year
        (mobile, email, or address)                   - 2-Minute process on MCA V3
      - Requires Class 3 DSC of Director              - Verified via Mobile & Email OTP
      - Requires Certification by CA / CS / CMA       - ZERO DSC & ZERO professional fee
```

| Parameter | Form DIR-3 KYC (e-Form) | DIR-3 KYC Web |
|---|---|---|
| **Filing Trigger** | First-time filing after DIN allotment, OR when mobile, email, or residential address changes | Subsequent years when personal data is **100% identical** to past filings |
| **Authentication Method** | **Class 3 DSC** of the director | **Mobile OTP + Email OTP** authentication |
| **Professional Certification** | Mandatory digital certification by practicing **CA, CS, or CMA** | **No professional certification needed** |
| **Supporting Attachments** | Self-attested PAN, Aadhaar, Passport, and address proof (< 2 months) | **Zero attachments required** |
| **Government Fee (Before Sept 30)** | **₹0 (Free)** | **₹0 (Free)** |

---

## The September 30 Statutory Deadline & ₹0 Government Fee

Every year, the statutory window for Director KYC opens on April 1 and closes on **September 30**:
- **On or before September 30:** The official government filing fee is **₹0 (Completely Free)**.
- **After September 30:** The MCA portal automatically locks the filing under the regular queue, moves the DIN to deactivated status, and charges an **additional statutory fee of ₹5,000**.

---

## The Cost of Default: Deactivation and the Flat ₹5,000 Late Fee

Under Section 403 and the Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014:
- The ₹5,000 penalty is **fixed and non-discretionary**.
- The MCA portal will not allow submission or processing of Form DIR-3 KYC without first generating a Challan of ₹5,000 via the Bharatkosh payment gateway.
- If a company has three directors who all miss the September 30 deadline, the combined penalty to restore their DINs is **₹15,000** ($3 \times ₹5,000$).

## Step-by-Step Procedure: Completing DIR-3 KYC Web (2-Minute Process)

If your mobile number, email ID, and address have not changed since your last filing:

1. **Log into MCA V3:** Access `mca.gov.in` under your registered business or user profile.
2. **Navigate to Service:** Click on **"MCA Services"** > **"Company Services"** > **"DIR-3-KYC-WEB"**.
3. **Input DIN:** Enter the 8-digit Director Identification Number.
4. **Pre-Populated Verification:** The portal fetches the registered mobile number and email ID (masked for security).
5. **Generate OTPs:** Click **"Send OTP"**. Two distinct OTPs are dispatched: one to your personal mobile number and one to your personal email address.
6. **Enter OTPs:** Enter both 6-digit OTPs in the respective fields within 15 minutes.
7. **Submit & Download Challan:** Click **"Submit"**. A zero-rupee **Service Request Number (SRN) Challan** is generated instantly confirming successful KYC for that financial year.

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure: Filing Form DIR-3 KYC (When Details Change)

If you changed your mobile number, email ID, or residential address, you cannot use the Web service; you must file the full e-form:

1. **Download Form DIR-3 KYC:** Download the latest offline web-form from the MCA V3 forms download page.
2. **Fill Updated Particulars:** Enter updated contact details, residential address, citizenship, and occupational status.
3. **Attach Self-Attested Documents:**
   - Copy of **PAN Card** (mandatorily self-attested by director).
   - Copy of **Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport**.
   - Current residential address proof (Bank statement or utility bill < 2 months old).
4. **Affix Director's DSC:** Sign using the director's Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate.
5. **Professional Certification:** Get the form certified by an independent practicing Chartered Accountant, Company Secretary, or Cost Accountant with active UDIN.
6. **Upload & Submit:** Upload to MCA V3 and download the acknowledgment receipt.

## How a Deactivated DIN Paralyzes Company Operations

Allowing a director's DIN to lapse into deactivated status has severe cascading effects on the business:

1. **Inability to Sign MCA E-Forms:** The MCA V3 portal blocks any form bearing a deactivated DIN. The company cannot file:
   - **Form AOC-4 (Financial Statements)**
   - **Form MGT-7 (Annual Return)**
   - **Form DIR-12 (Board appointments)**
2. **Compounding ₹100/Day Company Penalties:** Because the company cannot upload its annual filings without active director signatures, the company accumulates ₹100 per day per form in statutory late fees.
3. **Bank Account Verification Failures:** When corporate banks conduct periodic KYC updates under RBI mandates, a "Deactivated" DIN status on MCA master data can prompt banks to freeze corporate internet banking access.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a disqualified director still need to file DIR-3 KYC?
Yes. The statutory rule applies to every individual who holds an allotted Director Identification Number, regardless of whether the director is currently active, resigned, or disqualified under Section 164(2).

### Can two directors use the same mobile number or email address for DIR-3 KYC?
No. The MCA system enforces unique personal identifiers. Each director must provide a unique personal mobile number and personal email address; corporate group emails or shared numbers are rejected.

### What happens to a company if its director's DIN is deactivated?
A deactivated DIN cannot be used to digitally sign or upload any statutory form on the MCA portal (including annual filings AOC-4 and MGT-7). This effectively freezes the company's compliance, triggering ₹100/day penalties across other corporate forms.

### How long does it take for a DIN to become active again after paying the ₹5,000 late fee?
Once Form DIR-3 KYC is filed with the ₹5,000 additional fee and processed by the MCA V3 payment gateway, the DIN status typically changes back to 'Approved / Active' within 2 to 4 hours.
